# Code of Conduct

RelayDock is built by volunteers. This page says how we treat each other in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you assume.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Keep it about the project. Stay on topic in each issue and pull request.
- Help newcomers. Everyone filed a first bug report once.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's background, identity, body, age, religion or experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Posting stream keys, passwords or other credentials, yours or anyone else's.
- Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.

## What maintainers do

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues, and reject contributions that break these rules. For repeated or serious cases they may block a person from the project. They explain their decision when they act.

## Report a problem

Contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's Report content link on the comment. Say what happened and where. Maintainers keep the reporter's name private unless the reporter agrees otherwise.

## Scope

These rules apply in every project space and whenever someone represents the project in public.
